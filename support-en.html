What is free?

The 1930, 1934 and 1938 World Cups (the real tournament and the quiz), the Museum of Editions, How the rules changed, the Hall of Legends and your country’s journey. “Full History” unlocks all 23 editions and every quiz with a single purchase — no subscription, no ads.
