Privacy Policy

Football Finals Archive (iOS app) · last updated: 29 September 2026 · Română

In short

We do not collect any personal data. The app has no user account, no ads, no analytics, no tracking and no servers of its own.

What is stored on your device

  • your career progress, the Trophy Room, quiz records, your chosen theme and any country you picked manually;
  • whether you bought “Full History” (so the app also works offline).

This data stays on your phone only (UserDefaults), is never sent to us and is deleted together with the app. The country for “Your country’s journey” is worked out locally from your device’s region and language settings, without GPS location.

Payments

The “Full History” purchase is processed entirely by Apple through the App Store. We never receive or store payment details or your identity.

Children

The app collects no data from anyone, including children.

Third parties

We use no advertising, analytics or social media SDKs.
