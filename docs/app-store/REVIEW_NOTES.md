# App Review Information — text pentru „Notes” (în engleză, pentru reviewer)

Copiază blocul de mai jos în App Store Connect → versiunea aplicației → *App Review Information → Notes*.
Sign-in required: **No** (aplicația nu are cont).

```
Football Finals Archive (Romanian: "Arhiva Mondialelor") is an independently made educational game about the history of
international football championships, 1930–2026. It uses only historical facts (years, host countries, team and player names,
scores). It contains no third-party trademarks, logos, emblems, trophy designs, mascots, ball designs, photos, anthems or TV footage,
and no reference to any football governing body or its competition brands.

No account, no login, no ads, no analytics, no tracking. All progress is stored locally.

FREE CONTENT
- The main mode ("Relive a tournament": real results with goalscorers) and the edition quiz for the 1930, 1934, 1938 and 1994 editions
- Museum (edition stories), Rules evolution, Legends gallery, "Your country's path"

IN-APP PURCHASE (one non-consumable, Family Sharing enabled)
- Product: "Full History" — com.puiutpavel.historyofworldcup.fullhistory
- Unlocks every other tournament (1950–1990, 1998–2026) in the main mode and every quiz mode.

HOW TO REACH THE PURCHASE
1. Main menu → "Relive a tournament" → tap any edition from 1950 onward (marked 🔒)
   — or main menu → "Quiz" → "Maraton 1930 → 2026".
2. The "Full History" screen opens → "Unlock everything" → StoreKit purchase sheet.
Restore Purchases: main menu → "About & settings" → "Restore purchases".
It is also on the purchase screen.

The app follows the device language: English, Romanian, Spanish, Portuguese, German, French or Italian (English otherwise). Historical match data: Fjelstul football database by Joshua C. Fjelstul (CC-BY-SA 4.0), credited
in the app ("About & settings").
```

Înainte de trimitere: IAP-ul „Full History” trebuie să fie în starea *Ready to Submit* și **atașat la versiunea trimisă** (secțiunea *In-App Purchases and Subscriptions* a versiunii), altfel reviewerul nu îl poate cumpăra (respingere 2.1).
